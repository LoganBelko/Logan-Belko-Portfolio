When low utilization still feels crowded

A post-occupancy study of two campus recreation centers, one of them LEED Platinum. Machine counts said the gyms were mostly empty. The people using them said otherwise.

About

About me

I graduated from the University of Arizona in May 2026 with a B.S. in Sustainable Built Environments. My work combines field observation, survey research, and GIS to find where design intentions and real-world use don't line up, and what to do about it.

Before graduating, I spent nearly two years as a personal trainer at UA Campus Recreation. That's where my capstone question came from: why does a gym feel crowded when most of its machines are empty?

Outside of work, I'm usually hiking in a national park with a camera.
