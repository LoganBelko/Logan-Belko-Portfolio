Repair, Reconnect, Regenerate

Repair.Reconnect.Regenerate.

A restoration framework for a campus-edge neighborhood in Tucson, built on four weeks of field data and scaled in three steps, from fixing what's broken to imagining what's possible.

The neighborhood

Rincon Heights sits on the edge of the University of Arizona campus. A wash cuts through it: one of the few pieces of connected open ground left in the area, and the backbone of this study.

Because the neighborhood borders campus, the university is a stakeholder in what happens here, and a likely partner in any restoration.
